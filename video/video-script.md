# Product

mutande introduces **Address Intelligence**.

Every person. Every assistant. Every service. Every workflow.
Can have a trusted address inside an organisation.

```text
alice@salesco
alice@salesco/jarvis
alice@salesco/research
bob@salesco
finance@salesco
```

These are identities. Not implementations.
The runtime behind an address can change. The address does not.

---

## Three messages (Apple-style — on-screen copy)

1. **An address for every intelligence.**
2. **Send work to who should do it.**
3. **One message. The whole team.**

Visual beats prove each line. Encryption is plumbing only — never a fourth message.

Landing tagline (do not compete): **Address Intelligence.**
Supporting: Give every person, assistant and service in your organisation a trusted address. Route work by identity, not implementation.

# Format

- 1080 × 1080 · 60fps · ~28s · muted · seamless loop
- Implementations: Remotion (`video/`, primary ship) · Motion Canvas (`video-mc/`, diagram alternative)
- Subtle camera · cinematic but restrained
- Mood: calm, trustworthy, infrastructure, modern macOS (Linear / Tailscale / Apple / Arc / Notion)
- Not: crypto, neon AI, hacker movie, particle soup
- Palette: stone, paper, warm bronze, minimal black — no purple AI gradients

Every frame reinforces: **Intelligence has addresses.**

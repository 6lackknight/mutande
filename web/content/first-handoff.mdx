# First handoff

Send one encrypted handoff so the loop is real.

## In the Mac app

1. Confirm you’re signed in and connected to an AI host.
2. Optionally verify a colleague with a [safety number](/docs/safety-numbers).
3. Ask your agent to draft a short handoff — to a teammate (`bob@acme`), another of your agents (`@claude`), or the team (`@all@acme`).

## With your agent

Typical flow:

1. Stage questions or resources with draft tools.
2. Review the draft; confirm before send ([human decisions](/docs/agents/human-decisions)).
3. Forward to create a [thread](/docs/threads).
4. Watch status: open → replied → closed when done.

## What “done” looks like

- Sender sees the thread in the app.
- Recipient’s agent can list it under needs-action and reply on the same thread.
- Hub only carried ciphertext and routing metadata — see [security](/docs/security).

Deeper workflow: [agent workflow](/docs/agents/workflow).

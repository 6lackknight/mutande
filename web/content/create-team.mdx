# Create a team

Use this path when you’re starting a new org.

## Steps

1. Open the mutande Mac app (or [sign in on the web](/login)).
2. Sign in with your account.
3. Choose **Create a team**.
4. Pick an **org slug** (the part after `@` in handles, e.g. `acme`).
5. Confirm your **handle** (defaults from your email local-part, e.g. `you@acme`).

You’re in. Invite teammates from the web dashboard when you’re ready.

## Tips

- Org slug is user-picked and should be short and memorable.
- Your handle is how colleagues address your agent: `you@acme`.
- Keys are created on your Mac automatically — you don’t manage crypto files.

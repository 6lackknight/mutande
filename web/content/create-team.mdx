# Create an organization

Starting fresh? This path creates a closed **org** — your team's address namespace — so colleagues and agents know where to reach you. Pick a short slug (the part after `@` in handles); everyone in the org shares that suffix.

## Steps

1. Open the mutande Mac app (or [sign in on the web](/login)).
2. Sign in with your account (Auth0 — same account on Mac and web).
3. Choose **Create a team** (creates your org).
4. Pick an **org slug** (e.g. `acme` → handles like `you@acme`).
5. Confirm your **handle** (defaults from your email local-part, e.g. `you@acme`).

If you already joined an org on the web, the Mac app skips create/join and takes you straight to [Connect your AI](/docs/connect-ai).

You're in. Next the app blocks on Connect your AI, then the first-ping wizard — home stays gated until both MCP and skill are wired.

## Inviting colleagues

- Solo orgs: open the Mac **Contacts** tab — the invite CTA opens the web app at `/admin/invites`.
- Or go to [invites](/admin/invites) while signed in on the web.
- Colleagues appear in Contacts (copy handle / Message) once they join.

## Tips

- Org slug is user-picked — short and memorable beats clever.
- Your handle is how people address **you** in mutande (`you@acme`), not your login email.
- Keys are created on your Mac automatically — you don't manage crypto files.

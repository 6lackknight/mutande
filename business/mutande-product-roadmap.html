mutande
Prove the courier
before expanding the address.
product roadmap · aug 2026
Verdict: Product is ahead of evidence. Sequence the next 8 weeks around one proof. A working alpha exists — Mac + Windows, MCP + skill, Threads / Collab / Network, E2E sidecar. Demand, retention, and willingness to pay do not. The next release of work is not iOS, A2A, or more surface area. It is 20 qualified multi-tool users completing 100 real work handoffs.
Where we are
Shipped
Rolling alpha 2.0.x. Auth0, create/join org, host connect (MCP then skill), ping wizard, Threads split, Collab boards, Network, blobs, hosted MCP, Mac + Windows.
Unproven
Frequency of pain, unattended handoffs in real work, Day 7/30 return, invitations, E2E as a purchase reason, paid conversion.
ICP (wedge)
Solo or 2–5 person founding team. Two or more AI hosts in the same week. Card-payers. Mac first.
Evidence so far
1/5 pilots. Waitlist still small. Copy-paste is the stated workaround. Setup friction (MCP, Keychain, notifications) already shows up as the tax vs paste.
The sequencing rule
Gap

Day-one success in the app is a ping. The thesis is an unattended work handoff — agent A routes a useful packet to agent B without the human as courier. Pings will fill the funnel with false confidence.

Company vs wedge

Collab, Network, org addresses, and Address Intelligence are the company. The beachhead is personal multi-tool courier. Do not sell or expand the company until the wedge repeats.

Copy for this phase

Lead with “stop being the courier.” Do not lead with E2E, handles, or an unnamed category. Encryption is a product fact; it is not yet a purchase reason for this ICP.

North star
100 real handoffs
The only proof that unlocks the rest of this map

20 people who already use at least two AI tools, through 100 useful work packets that move without the human copying context. A ping does not count. Neither does a demo they do not return to.

1 — Courier
Next ~8 weeks · prove the wedge
in focus

Make the first unattended work handoff the onboarding magic moment, then get 20 qualified users through 100 of them.

01

Redefine first-run. Ping can stay as a connectivity check. Complete-when should be a real packet moving between two of the user’s hosts (personal @all or @claude ←→ Cursor) without copy-paste. Celebrate that, not Pong.

02

Cut the tax vs paste. MCP + skill + Keychain + notifications is the reason people stay on Cmd+C. One-host connect that actually works, then a second host, then the handoff. Assisted onboarding is a fail for the proof threshold.

03

Instrument the magic event. Track: hosts connected, first work handoff (not ping), successful handoffs/week, manual interventions, Day 7 / Day 30 return, failures. Mixpanel is the work-vs-ping split. Ops Overview is the hub census beside it: users/20, multi-host, replied threads/100 (pings included), Day 7/30, team orgs. Do not treat waitlist or ping-Pong as proof.

04

Recruit for behaviour, not enthusiasm. 20 people already using ≥2 AI tools in one workflow. Baseline their current courier loop before install. Follow up after real usage, not after onboarding.

05

Talk like the wedge. Build in public about the tab loop. DM searchable complainers. Seed solo power users. Do not sell teams, addresses, or encryption yet.

2 — Repeat & invite
Only if Phase 1 hits · retention is the product
gated

Users return because mutande is part of a recurring workflow, then pull a second identity in.

01

Make the second handoff obvious. Inbox at new chat already exists. Tighten so returning to a host surfaces waiting work without a timer poll or a human courier.

02

Invitation as the first network test. Measure whether people add another host, another of their own agents, or a cofounder because the workflow needs them — not because onboarding asked.

03

Trust after value. Now E2E and safety numbers can show up as why they keep using it. Still not the homepage headline.

3 — Address network
Only if invitations create incremental value
gated

Handles, orgs, and Network stop being scaffolding and become the reason a second person installs.

01

Land-and-expand for real. Solo users pull cofounders. Cofounders pull contractors. Cold team sales stay off. Org invite UX earns its keep here, not in Phase 1.

02

Collab as a consequence. Boards are already shipped. Promote them when people have threads worth filing — not as the way in.

03

Name the category only after people use the addresses. alice@acme/cursor is the artifact. “Agent address layer” is internal until unaided users say something like it.

4 — Platform
After network value · iOS, A2A, pay
later

Same encrypted mail on more devices; mutande as the product layer on A2A; money after value.

01

Charge after the workflow is habitual. Separate stated WTP from a paid conversion. Free inline / premium blobs can stay the model — do not invent agent-native pricing until people repeat.

02

iOS companion (PRD v1.5). Humans read/reply on the phone; agents stay on desktop. Multi-device wrap is already in the crypto seam. Do not start this to “complete the product” before Phase 1.

03

A2A-compatible, not A2A-competing. Mail client to their SMTP. Stance now; thin discovery (Agent Cards) when Phase 3 is real. Full protocol work is not the next 8 weeks.

Proof gates
To unlock Must see Otherwise
Phase 2 Most of the 20 reach a work handoff without assisted onboarding. Repeated handoffs/week, not one demo. Keep cutting setup tax. Do not add Collab/iOS/A2A.
Phase 3 Day 7 return. Users name a workflow they no longer paste. At least some invite a second identity unprompted. Valuable courier, weak network — stay personal, revisit the address thesis.
Phase 4 pay Habitual use, then an actual paid conversion — not a survey. Keep the wedge free longer; pricing is wrong, not necessarily the product.
Phase 4 iOS Desktop mail is already a habit. Humans need to reply away from the Mac. Phone is a companion, not a growth engine. Wait.
Do not build yet

iOS / Android — PRD v1.5 is correct later. It does not help 100 desktop handoffs.

A2A implementation — Need a written stance and a compatibility sketch. Not a protocol port.

Leading with E2E or category ownership — Internal true, GTM false for this ICP.

New hosts beyond Cursor / Claude / ChatGPT desktop — Only if a qualified user is blocked from the magic moment. Browser agents stay the weaker hosted-MCP path until the desktop wedge repeats.

Enterprise / compliance / audit productization — That’s LangSmith’s gravity. Don’t follow it to look serious.

Kill / pivot signals

They understand the demo and go back to paste — automation doesn’t save enough time vs setup. Product problem, not marketing.

Handoffs are too rare to justify a dedicated app — the pain is real but not frequent. Don’t expand the thesis to rescue it.

They won’t let agents talk without supervising every send — courier stays the human. mutande becomes a nicer pasteboard; that’s a different company.

They use it, invite no one, and still won’t pay — personal tool maybe; address network no. Stay in Phase 2 and change the bet.

Watch while shipping Phase 1
⚠ Native host handoff — If Claude Desktop or Cursor ships cross-host handoff, the sidecar becomes a compatibility layer. This outranks Ando. Keep a fast response: stay the address + E2E + org layer they don’t want to own.
⚠ AgentMessenger skill install — Same ICP, addresses, E2E threads, Cursor/Claude skill. Cheaper to try than a daemon. Win on org-shaped mail and an actual Mac app, not on “we also have addresses.”
⚠ Ando BYO agents — They are closer than “replaces your tools.” They are still team-chat-first. Don’t wander into rebuilding Slack.
⚠ KV fresh-start window — Stay on Deno KV through Phase 1. Ops storage strip flips when a 2+ member org, a published listing, or a non-zero ledger appears. Then run postgres.prd before Stripe — do not migrate as part of proving the wedge.
⚠ A2A v1.0 adoption — Linux Foundation, 150+ orgs. Write the compatibility stance this quarter even if code waits.
